About Me

I'm a 19-year-old game developer from Ukraine, currently based in England. I moved to the UK in 2022 due to the war, and have been building my future here since — through self-study, determination, and a lot of C++.

My gap year was spent almost entirely self-studying game development. It wasn't always easy — I went through multiple burnouts along the way — but I pushed through and eventually built a daily schedule that works perfectly for me. The projects on this portfolio are ones I built entirely on my own, specifically to demonstrate what I'm capable of independently.

My focus is low-level systems, procedural generation, and game mechanics — the kind of work that makes virtual worlds feel genuinely alive. I'm a VR enthusiast with a deep interest in what Unreal Engine can do at the edges of interactive experience. Always building, always learning.

Project — 01

Procedural World Gen

A 2D side-scrolling world generator built entirely from scratch in C++ using raylib. Features fully procedural terrain with distinct biomes (mountains, plains, desert), Perlin worm cave systems, underground dungeons, and dynamically generated structures — every world is unique, with no pre-authored content. Currently still in active development.

Project — 02

Last Bite Standing

A 3D survival game built in Unreal Engine 5 using C++, completed within a single week. Players and AI characters spawn on an open map where everyone slowly starves — food spawners are scattered across the world and the last survivor wins. Features complete survival mechanics, AI enemy behaviour, live leaderboard tracking, and a full game loop. Built almost entirely in C++ with minimal Blueprint.
